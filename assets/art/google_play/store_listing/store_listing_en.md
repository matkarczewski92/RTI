## Full description

Reptile Tycoon is a cozy idle tycoon game about building your own reptile center. Develop Green Meadow, buy habitats, collect reptiles, assign them to care spaces, and watch your center start earning R$.

Care for reptiles by feeding them, giving them water, cleaning their habitats, and playing with them. Discover variants, grow your collection, complete quests, claim achievements, and improve your player progression.

The game is built for short mobile sessions. Return for offline progress, expand your habitats, and gradually build a cheerful reptile collection. MVP progress is saved locally on the device.

The current MVP includes:

- one playable biome,
- habitats,
- reptile purchase,
- reptile assignment,
- reptile management,
- care actions,
- active income,
- offline progress,
- quests,
- achievements,
- workers and upgrades if available in the build,
- local progress save.
