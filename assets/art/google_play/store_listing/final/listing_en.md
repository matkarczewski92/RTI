# Reptile Tycoon - listing EN

## App title

Reptile Tycoon

## Short description

Build your reptile center, upgrade habitats, discover variants and earn R$.

## Full description

Reptile Tycoon is a friendly idle tycoon game about running your own reptile center. Develop the first biome, buy and build habitats, collect reptiles, assign them to care spaces, and grow your collection step by step.

Each reptile can help your center earn R$, and care matters. Manage feeding, water, cleanliness and happiness so your center can keep growing. Discover variants, complete quests, claim achievements, and improve your player progression.

The game is built for short mobile sessions. Return for offline progress, check your habitats, grow your collection, and gradually upgrade your center. The current MVP runs locally: it does not require a user account, does not connect progress to a server, and saves the game on the device.
